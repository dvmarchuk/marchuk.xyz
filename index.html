What I build

Four kinds of jobs I take on

Most projects are one of these. If yours is something else, ask anyway. If I'm not the right person, I'll say so on the first call.

Automate the weekly chore

The thing someone on your team does every Monday: reposting, copying data between two systems, updating prices, chasing a spreadsheet.

e.g. Facebook listing sync, inventory publishing, price matching

Answer leads when you can't

AI assistants that draft replies to Marketplace, Messenger, and email leads using your real inventory and hours. You approve, it sends. Fully hands-off once you trust it.

Connect systems that don't talk

Your DMS, your website, your titling system, your CRM. I make them share data so nobody types the same customer in three times.

e.g. photo of an ID and a vehicle link fills out the deal in two systems

Small sites and internal tools

A direct-booking site, a dashboard, a simple internal app. Built plain, hosted cheap, easy for you to keep running.

e.g. vacation rental booking site with calendar sync

Recent projects

Used car dealer, Virginia

Facebook Marketplace inventory sync

Live
ProblemAround 160 vehicles listed individually on Marketplace and in Facebook groups. Listings drifted from the dealer's website: sold cars still up, new arrivals missing, prices out of date, old posts buried.
BuiltA Claude-driven agent that reads the dealer's inventory site as the source of truth, marks sold vehicles, publishes new ones with photos to Marketplace and the groups, matches prices to the site, and renews stale listings. Shows its plan, gets one approval, runs.
ChangedThe weekly repost-by-hand routine went away. Listings stay accurate without anyone checking.
