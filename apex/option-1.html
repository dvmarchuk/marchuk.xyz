What a security system does for you

The benefits look a little different at home and at work.

For your home

  • Peace of mindKnow your home and family are covered, whether you're upstairs or out of town.
  • Deter crimeVisible cameras and alarms make your home a harder target.
  • Lower home insuranceMany insurers discount premiums for homes with security systems. Ask your agent.
  • Remote monitoringCheck your cameras from your phone, wherever you are.

For your business

  • Protect your assetsCover equipment, inventory, and property inside and out.
  • Protect customers and staffA secure, well watched space is safer for everyone who walks in.
  • Run things more smoothlySee what's happening across your business without being on site.
  • Cut costsFewer losses from theft and damage, and possible insurance savings.
